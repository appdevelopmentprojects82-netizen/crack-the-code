HOW TO PLAY
  1. Guess the secret code: type the digits and press ✓. No digit is used twice.
  2. 7 right digit, right place
    3 in the code, wrong place
    5 not in the code
  3. You have 4 tries, plus 2 locked tries you can open with an ad or 100 . Each win pays 40 .
  4. Losing a level costs 1 ❤️. You have 5 lives; one comes back every 4 hours, or refill all 5 for 1500 .
  5. 💡 Hint shows one correct digit in its place. 3 hints cost 700 .
  6. 🎁 Daily targets: win 5, 10, 15 and 20 levels in a day for up to 100 .
  7. 🔒 A padlocked box hides its colours until you find the digit in the box with the 🔑.
    🕸️ A spider web hides its colours until you have found 3 other digits.
  8. From level 101 codes have 5 digits, from level 201 6 digits.
