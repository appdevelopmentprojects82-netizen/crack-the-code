HOW TO PLAY
  1. Guess the secret code: type the digits and press ✓. No digit is used twice.
  2. 7 right digit, right place
    3 in the code, wrong place
    5 not in the code
  3. You have 4 tries. Each win pays 40 🪙 — watch an ad to double it.
  4. Losing a level costs 1 ❤️. You have 5 lives; one comes back every 30 minutes, or refill all 5 for 1500 🪙.
  5. 💡 Hint shows one correct digit in its place. 3 hints cost 700 🪙.
  6. 🔒 A padlocked box hides its colours until you find the digit in the box with the 🔑.
    🕸️ A spider web hides its colours until you have found 3 other digits.
  7. From level 101 codes have 5 digits, from level 201 6 digits.
